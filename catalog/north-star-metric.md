# north-star-metric

**Purpose:** Choose a North Star Metric and a small set of supporting input metrics.

**Invocation:** automatic

**Use when:** The product needs an explicit measure of delivered value and a coherent measurement framework.

**Do not use when:** Calculating an already-defined metric, debugging analytics ingestion, market sizing or assuming correlation proves causality.

**Overlaps and default:** product-strategy includes metrics at a high level; this provides the focused framework. create-prd connects feature outcomes to the agreed product measurement.

**Requirements:** Product value proposition, business model and user behavior context. Data access is necessary to validate proposed measures, but not to draft candidates with stated assumptions.

**Expected output:** A proposed North Star, supporting inputs and rationale against the skill's criteria.

**Positive example:** "Define a North Star Metric for our collaboration product and its supporting inputs."

**Negative example:** "Fix the SQL query that computes our existing weekly-active-user metric."
