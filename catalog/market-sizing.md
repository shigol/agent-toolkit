# market-sizing

**Purpose:** Estimate TAM, SAM and SOM using defined boundaries and top-down/bottom-up reasoning.

**Invocation:** automatic

**Use when:** Sizing an addressable market, comparing a market-entry opportunity or preparing sourced market estimates.

**Do not use when:** General competitor research without a sizing question, internal usage analysis, or presenting assumptions as verified market facts.

**Overlaps and default:** Can supply evidence for product-strategy or create-prd. It owns market-size estimation, not the whole strategy or customer-interview process.

**Requirements:** Product/market boundaries, geography, segments and relevant unit economics. Reliable current sources or supplied research; cite data and mark assumptions, dates and uncertainty.

**Expected output:** A sourced TAM/SAM/SOM estimate with reconciled methods, assumptions and risks.

**Positive example:** "Estimate the addressable market for this product in the UK using cited sources."

**Negative example:** "Investigate why our current users abandon the signup form."
