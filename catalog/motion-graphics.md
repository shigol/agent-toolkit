# motion-graphics

**Purpose:** Create a short unnarrated composition where movement carries the message.

**Invocation:** automatic

**Use when:** A logo sting, kinetic title, chart/stat hit, animated map or standalone overlay is the requested deliverable, typically under ten seconds.

**Do not use when:** Narrated or longer multi-scene storytelling, simple captions over footage, static title cards or interactive application motion.

**Overlaps and default:** Enter through hyperframes. Use its route contract to distinguish specialized workflows; general-video handles custom pieces outside this short motion-first scope. Read core/CLI guidance only as needed.

**Requirements:** Brief and suitable assets; bundled category/director guides. The pinned workflow names macOS Apple Silicon or Linux x64, plus Node/FFmpeg and CLI checks. Some assets/providers require separate access; instructions do not supply it.

**Expected output:** A checked short composition and preview; encoded MP4/overlay when the render step is authorized.

**Positive example:** "Make an eight-second animated logo sting with no voice-over."

**Negative example:** "Produce a narrated two-minute explanation with several scenes."
