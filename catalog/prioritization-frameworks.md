# prioritization-frameworks

**Purpose:** Select and explain an appropriate method for comparing problems or initiatives.

**Invocation:** automatic

**Route:** Prioritize product work

**Use when:** Choosing between RICE, ICE, opportunity scoring and other frameworks, or applying a method to supplied inputs.

**Do not use when:** Inventing scores from absent evidence, diagnosing a production outage or replacing the entire product strategy.

**Overlaps and default:** product-strategy establishes direction; this helps make a bounded prioritization decision. create-prd documents an initiative after its scope is chosen.

**Requirements:** Decision context and, for scoring, the required evidence/estimates. The bundled reference is sufficient for explanation; external templates are optional. Label estimates and avoid fabricated precision.

**Expected output:** A justified method and, when inputs exist, a transparent comparison or scoring table.

**Positive example:** "Help us choose RICE or ICE for these initiatives and explain the tradeoffs."

**Negative example:** "Explain why yesterday's API outage occurred."
