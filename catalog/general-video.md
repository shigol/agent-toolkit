# general-video

**Purpose:** Author or edit a custom HyperFrames video when a more specialized route does not fit.

**Invocation:** automatic

**Route:** Video production techniques

**Use when:** The selected workflow is general-video: custom multi-scene pieces, montages, footage remixes, static loops/cards or a brief specifying the companion flow.

**Do not use when:** Automatically taking every video request, a short motion-first logo sting, plain captions, or a specialized product-launch workflow.

**Overlaps and default:** hyperframes chooses the workflow first. motion-graphics owns short unnarrated motion-first units. Use the bundled source adapters and core/CLI references for the chosen stage.

**Requirements:** A routed brief, project state and relevant assets. Node/FFmpeg/HyperFrames runtime for production; source/provider access when required. Preserve toolkit pins instead of running upstream skill-refresh instructions.

**Expected output:** A custom composition, reviewed preview and authorized final render, with required media and visual checks.

**Positive example:** "Remix these clips into a custom montage; no specialized workflow fits."

**Negative example:** "Create a five-second animated chart hit without narration."
