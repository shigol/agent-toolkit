# product-strategy

**Purpose:** Develop a coherent Product Strategy Canvas covering who to serve, how to win and what to leave out.

**Invocation:** automatic

**Route:** Set product strategy or direction

**Use when:** Defining product direction, positioning, target segments, growth approach, capabilities or defensibility.

**Do not use when:** A single implementation ticket, superficial prose polishing or an isolated market-size calculation.

**Overlaps and default:** Use market-sizing for size evidence, north-star-metric for focused measurement work and prioritization-frameworks for bounded choices. create-prd translates an initiative into requirements.

**Requirements:** Product/current positioning, customer and competitive evidence, resources and constraints. Separate hypotheses from facts; obtain missing evidence through appropriate research tools.

**Expected output:** A nine-part strategy canvas with explicit tradeoffs, coherent choices and validation experiments.

**Positive example:** "Define our product strategy for this segment, including what we will not pursue."

**Negative example:** "Adjust the spacing and typography of the pricing screen."
